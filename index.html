      07 — Process

      How a world gets built.

      1. 01

        Map

        A short call and a written brief: who arrives, what they need, and what the site must make them do.

      2. 02

        Sketch

        Wireframes, then a design direction with real type, colour and motion studies. You see it moving before it’s built.

      3. 03

        Build

        Hand-built front end, CMS or Shopify set-up, 3D and animation — tested on real phones, not just a big monitor.

      4. 04

        Launch

        Domain, analytics, speed and SEO checks, a handover video, and a month of fixes included after go-live.
